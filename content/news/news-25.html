Signature Global Share Price Target 2025: Brokerage sees strong growth in Gurugram market; predicts Rs 17000 cr GDV in FY26 – BUY, SELL or HOLD?

Powered By - FamilyandFlats • June 8, 2025




Signature Global Share Price: Brokerage firm Axis Securities is positive on the shares of Signature Global. The broking firm said that the real estate company continues to focus on the mid-income premium housing along with a low-cost land bank strategy to consolidate its market share. It also stated that the company’s successful transition to premium housing has sustained its sales momentum and strong operating cash flow (OCF). Shares of Signature Global climbed 2.77 percent and closed at Rs 1281 apiece on Friday. According to the NSE, this realty stock has a total market cap of Rs 17999.42 crore.

Signature Global Share Price Target 2025: Axis Securities has maintained a BUY rating with a target price of Rs 1330 (in the upcoming 6 to 9 months). In its report on May 31, the brokerage firm said, “Its strategy of focusing on key micro markets has proven to yield good results, aided by a strong realisation surge in the Gurugram markets. Considering Gurugram’s growth story and Signature’s ability to gain market share in these markets, we remain positive on this stock.” It also stated that the realty major plans to launch phase 2 of the Titanium project in Sector 71 and Deluxe-DXP in Sector 37D. “To continue its launch trajectory, it incurred business development expenses of Rs 1,060 Cr and acquired ~8 Mn sq. ft. and a GDV of Rs 12,500 Cr. Signature continues to focus on its key areas of micro markets of SPR, Dwarka Expressway, and Sohna Corridor. For FY25, the company launched five projects with a GDV worth Rs 13,800 Cr. For the upcoming year, it plans to launch GDV worth Rs 17,000 Cr (~23% growth), with ~Rs 10,000-11,000 Cr being launched in H1FY26," it added.

Signature Global Share Price History: Signature Global (India) Limited is a component of the BSE 500. According to the BSE analytics (as of June 8), shares of Signature Global gave a positive return of 5.61 per cent and 7.24 per cent in the last 1 week and 2 weeks, respectively. Shares of the company also gained 13.17 per cent and 26.26 per cent in the last 1 month and 3 months, respectively. In the past 1 year, shares of the realty firm rose 4 per cent.
