Southern Peripheral Road: All About Gurugram's Most Important Real Estate Corridor

Powered By - FamilyandFlats • May 31, 2025




Gurugram’s Southern Peripheral Road (SPR) has transformed into a prime real estate corridor in Delhi-NCR, connecting key areas like Sector 58 and NH-48. It features a mix of residential and commercial developments, with significant projects enhancing the skyline, including BPTP Freedom Park Life and Emaar Palm Drive.

Once a relatively quiet stretch, Gurugram’s Southern Peripheral Road (SPR) has swiftly emerged as one of the most sought-after real estate corridors in Delhi-NCR. SPR is an arterial road that starts near Sector 58 on the Gurgaon-Faridabad Road and connects to NH-48 near Sector 74A, intersecting Sohna Road at Badshahpur Chowk along the way.

A Blend of Residential and Commercial Development

A thriving residential and commercial centre is developing along the SPR corridor. Sectors 74, 74A, and 75A are the hubs of commercial and industrial activity. With notable projects like BPTP Freedom Park Life, Satya The Legend, Unitech The Close, Unitech Woodstock Floors, Emaar Palm Drive, and Tata Housing Primanti already influencing the skyline, the real estate landscape consists of both builder floors and high-rise apartments.

Strong Physical Infrastructure

SPR serves as an important link connecting Golf Course Road, Sohna Road and NH-48, making it one of the best-connected corridors in South Gurgaon. Additionally, seamless access to Cyber City is possible via the Sector 55-56 Rapid Metro station, while the underpasses on Golf Course Road have improved travel time to the IGI Airport.

Social & retail infra

The micro-market has a mix of developed as well as developing social and retail infrastructure. A number of schools, hospitals, shopping centres, restaurants and banks, can be accessed at a drive of 10-15 minutes. Key amenities include DPS International School, St. Xaviers High School, Scottish High International School, Artemis Hospital, W Pratiksha Hospital, Park Hospital, Vatika City Market, Omaxe Gurgaon Mall, Aeros City Square, etc.
