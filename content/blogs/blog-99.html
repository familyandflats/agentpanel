What makes Golf Course Extension Road stand out is its strong potential for property appreciation. Due to continuous infrastructure upgrades and increasing demand, property values here have shown steady growth. Surrounded by top schools, hospitals, shopping malls, and entertainment zones, residents enjoy a comfortable and well-connected lifestyle. The presence of green spaces and low-density planning also ensures a peaceful living environment.
