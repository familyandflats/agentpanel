Golf Course Extension: Luxury Apartments and Group Housing Projects

Powered By - FamilyandFlats • June 27, 2025



Gurgaon’s real estate market has undergone a transformative shift over the last decade, with Golf Course Extension Road (GCER) emerging as one of the most sought-after residential corridors. Strategically located and exceptionally well-planned, this stretch has become synonymous with luxury living, premium infrastructure, and cutting-edge residential projects. Whether you're a high-net-worth individual looking for a signature address or a growing family desiring world-class amenities, GCER offers a wide spectrum of options — from high-rise luxury apartments to thoughtfully designed group housing communities.

Why Golf Course Extension Road?


Golf Course Extension Road, often considered the extension of the already elite Golf Course Road, connects key sectors like 61, 62, 63, 65, 66, 67, 68, and 69. It offers seamless connectivity to Sohna Road, Southern Peripheral Road (SPR), and NH-48. The upcoming connectivity with the Central Peripheral Road and the Delhi-Mumbai Industrial Corridor (DMIC) further enhances its appeal. With the rapid development of infrastructure, metro extensions, wide arterial roads, and proximity to top schools, hospitals, and business hubs, the region is attracting both end-users and investors alike.

The Rise of Luxury Apartments

Luxury apartments along GCER have redefined the skyline of Gurgaon. Developers like DLF, M3M, Smart World, Emaar, IREO, Elan, and Conscient have launched world-class projects that offer more than just homes—they offer an aspirational lifestyle. These apartments often come equipped with private balconies, smart home features, imported fittings, modular kitchens, and expansive views. Amenities are no longer just about swimming pools and clubhouses. Today’s luxury apartments include wellness centers, spa facilities, rooftop lounges, concierge services, pet parks, and co-working spaces. Projects such as DLF The Arbour, M3M Golf Estate, IREO Skyon, and Smart World Orchard are prime examples of what new-age luxury means in Gurugram. Buyers in this segment include CXOs, NRIs, startup founders, and well-off professionals seeking a mix of comfort, security, and brand prestige. The 3BHK and 4BHK formats dominate, often priced between ?3 crore to ?8 crore, depending on size, view, and floor elevation.

Investment Appeal and Future Potential

Golf Course Extension Road is more than a residential destination—it is a growth corridor. With infrastructural upgrades such as underpasses, flyovers, and metro line extensions, the livability and capital appreciation potential of this area are high. The ongoing and proposed commercial developments along the corridor are expected to create thousands of new jobs, further boosting housing demand. Luxury properties here have already seen 15–20% price appreciation in the past 2 years, and experts predict continued momentum due to limited supply and sustained demand. The entry of global brands in hospitality and retail also indicates that the region is evolving into a lifestyle destination.

Conclusion

Golf Course Extension Road is fast becoming the epitome of modern urban living in Gurugram. From opulent skyscrapers to premium group housing societies, this corridor offers something for every discerning buyer. Excellent connectivity, future-ready infrastructure, top-tier amenities, and a strong investment outlook make GCER a compelling choice for both homeowners and investors.
